\section{HEP Packaging Coordination}\label{sec:hep-packaging-coordination}

Based off of evidence from work by the LHCb collaboration to use conda-forge packages of LHCb software for experiment use, the \href{https://hep-packaging-coordination.github.io/.github/}{HEP Packaging Coordination} community project~\cite{hep_packaging_coordination} on GitHub was created with the goal of creating multi-platform builds of HEP software for public use on conda-forge.
As of September 2026, HEP Packaging Coordination has over 120 packages from the broader HEP software community packaged and distributed through conda-forge with contributions from members of the ATLAS, CMS, LHCb, LEGEND, Belle II, SHiP, and DUNE experimental collaboration communities, as well as contributions from the Scikit-HEP, IRIS-HEP, ROOT Team, DIRAC, and CEDAR software organizations/collaborations, and from the HEP theory and phenomenology communities.
